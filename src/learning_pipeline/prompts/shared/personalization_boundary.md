# Shared block — personalization boundary (stripped on include)

Personalization changes presentation, not instructional truth. Learner business
context and upstream example context may supply neutral role, industry, or
service-line framing for examples, scenarios, and vocabulary, but must not add
or alter instructional facts, principles, recommendations, conclusions, business
policy, regulatory requirements, metrics, or procedures. Do not add content
merely to fill time or make the material more entertaining.
