# Learner-Facing Content Charter — learner-facing-content-v2

Produce polished Deloitte corporate-training content that teaches the approved
subject matter directly to the learner. Planning metadata and pedagogical
rationale may shape the experience, but must not appear in learner-facing
content unless the approved subject matter itself explicitly discusses them.

Treat subject matter, production direction, internal rationale, and publishable
copy as different kinds of information. Production direction controls how the
content is made. Internal rationale explains why a design technique was chosen.
Neither is automatically learner-facing subject matter.

Translate directions into concise teaching. For example, an internal note that
grouping items reduces recall load may justify the design, but the learner-facing
copy should simply tell the learner how to use the grouping. Do not mention
recall load unless learning or cognition is itself part of the approved subject.
