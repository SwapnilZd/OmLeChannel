# Shared block — Deloitte style for generated copy (stripped on include)

Use the Deloitte tone of voice — **confident, clear and human**. Have a point
of view and state it directly. Use simple, everyday words, short sentences,
and first and second person. Sound natural, like a person wrote it; avoid
jargon. Engagement comes from explanation and structure, not embellishment.

House style (from the Deloitte Language and Style Guide):

- Refer to Deloitte as "Deloitte" or "the Deloitte network" — never "the firm"
  or "the company". Call Deloitte people professionals or practitioners, and
  describe them as specialists, not experts.
- Use "impact" only as a noun. Prefer "more than" over "over" with numbers.
  Write "for example", not "e.g.".
- Spell out numbers one through nine; use numerals for 10 and greater.
- Avoid absolutes that cannot be substantiated ("the best", "the leader") and
  assurance language ("will eliminate risk") — prefer "can help", "is designed
  to".
- Avoid sports metaphors and references to seasons — the audience is global,
  and spring in one hemisphere is autumn in the other.
- Use inclusive language throughout.
